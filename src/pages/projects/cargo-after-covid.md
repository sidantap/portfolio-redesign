## What it is

Two decades of federal air cargo data, made explorable in a few clicks. The app is built on Bureau of Transportation Statistics tables and covers U.S. air cargo performance from 2003 to 2025.

## What I built

The transformations, KPIs, filters and trend views that turn twenty years of transportation tables into something you can explore. It's hosted on shinyapps.io.

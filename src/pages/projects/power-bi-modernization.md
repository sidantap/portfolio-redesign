## What it is

The utility's reporting ran on Crystal Reports. I moved 40 to 50 of them onto validated SQL Server views and Power BI semantic models, set up on-premises gateways and refresh schedules, and handled workspace access and distribution for each department. Along the way I fixed reports that ran in the designer but failed inside Lucity Web.

## New dashboards

On my own, I built 10 to 20 Power BI reports and data models (Power Query, DAX) for HR, Legal, Engineering, IT, Customer Service, Finance and Operations, with KPI frameworks and source-to-target mappings across SAP, HR, Finance and Engineering data.

## Impact

Together with the migration, the dashboards remove 20 to 40 hours of manual reporting a week.

## CO2 emissions

A Tableau Cloud CO2 emissions dashboard built from carrier and transportation data in Turvo, the company's transportation platform, with trend analysis and forecasting. It contributed to a 12.4% overall drop in emissions.

## Profitability

Customer profitability scorecards from P&L data, revenue forecasting for top and lower-ranked carriers, and a regional dashboard comparing gross profit across 4 regions and 49 states. It contributed to a 15% rise in overall profitability.

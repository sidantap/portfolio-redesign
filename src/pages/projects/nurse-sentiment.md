## The question

First-year nurses talk openly about their jobs in online forums. What do those posts say about stress, depression, the workplace and wellbeing, and how does that change over time?

## The pipeline

A Python pipeline scrapes the forum posts, cleans and lemmatizes the text, and classifies each post into four themes: stress, depression, workplace and wellbeing. An R Shiny dashboard shows how sentiment changes over time.

## Why it interests me

Getting meaning out of messy, unstructured language is the part of AI I find most interesting. The work supports a planned conference paper.

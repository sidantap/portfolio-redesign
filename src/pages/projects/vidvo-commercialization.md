## The fellowship

Kentucky Commercialization Ventures trains the people who take Kentucky's university research and inventions to market. I was one of ten fellows in the Fall 2025 cohort.

## The work

Concept development for VIDVO: customer discovery, a SWOT analysis, a failure-mode assessment, an opportunity evaluation, and a commercialization plan with business recommendations.

## What it is

Conditional Power Automate flows for the pilot Master Training Schedule. Each pilot moves through six training statuses, from New Entry to Training Complete, and each change triggers the next approval downstream.

## The rules

Curriculum rules are built in, such as simulator timing windows at 150 and 30 days. I coordinated with Training Records, Scheduling and the training staff to get them right.

## Related work

I also helped automate passport, visa and immigration reporting and crew scheduling flows, part of roughly 25 to 30 staff hours a week saved across stations.

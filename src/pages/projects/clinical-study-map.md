## What it is

An interactive R Shiny map of where clinical studies run around the world. It combines several data sources into one view that researchers and the proposal team use for RFP work.

## Around it

At Medpace I also built dbt and SQL transformations on Snowflake, cleaned and validated millions of clinical trial records with Python, SQL and R, pulled data from Medidata, built Power BI dashboards on Snowflake, and led monthly dashboard design sessions for 10+ analysts in the US and UK.
